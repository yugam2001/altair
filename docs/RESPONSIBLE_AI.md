# Responsible AI

## ALTAIR's Commitment

At ALTAIR, we believe Artificial Intelligence should empower learners by providing structured guidance while remaining transparent about its limitations. The goal of ALTAIR is not to replace human judgment, teachers, mentors, or career advisors, but to help users explore personalized learning pathways more efficiently.

Responsible AI principles are integrated throughout the product, from prompt design to user experience.

---

# Purpose of AI

ALTAIR uses a Large Language Model (LLM) to generate personalized learning roadmaps based on information provided by the user, including:

* Career Goal
* Current Skill Level
* Weekly Study Hours
* Preferred Learning Style
* Target Completion Timeline

The generated roadmap is intended to serve as educational guidance rather than a guaranteed plan for success.

---

# Transparency

ALTAIR clearly communicates that:

* Roadmaps are generated using AI.
* Recommendations are probabilistic, not absolute.
* Learning paths may not suit every individual.
* Users should adapt recommendations to their personal goals, circumstances, and available resources.

AI-generated content should be treated as a starting point rather than a final answer.

---

# Human-Centered Design

ALTAIR is designed to support human decision-making, not replace it.

Users remain in control of:

* What goals they pursue
* Which resources they use
* How quickly they progress
* Whether to modify the generated roadmap

The AI provides guidance, while the user makes the final decisions.

---

# Limitations

Although AI can generate useful recommendations, it has important limitations.

ALTAIR may:

* Recommend resources that become outdated over time.
* Produce imperfect or incomplete learning sequences.
* Miss emerging technologies or recent industry trends.
* Generate recommendations that require additional verification.

Users are encouraged to validate important educational and career decisions using trusted sources.

---

# Bias Awareness

Large Language Models are trained on diverse internet-scale datasets that may contain biases or incomplete information.

As a result, generated recommendations may unintentionally reflect:

* Geographic bias
* Language bias
* Industry bias
* Educational bias

ALTAIR does not assume AI outputs are universally correct and encourages users to critically evaluate all recommendations.

---

# Privacy

The MVP version of ALTAIR is designed with privacy in mind.

* User inputs are used only to generate the requested roadmap.
* Personal information is not collected unless explicitly required.
* No unnecessary user data is stored.
* Authentication and user accounts are intentionally excluded from the MVP.

Future versions will continue to prioritize responsible data handling.

---

# Safety

ALTAIR focuses exclusively on educational guidance.

The application is **not** intended to provide:

* Medical advice
* Legal advice
* Financial advice
* Mental health counseling
* Emergency assistance

Users seeking guidance in these areas should consult qualified professionals.

---

# Responsible Prompt Engineering

Prompt design within ALTAIR follows these principles:

* Generate structured and actionable learning plans.
* Avoid presenting uncertain information as fact.
* Encourage practical, project-based learning.
* Recommend verification where appropriate.
* Maintain a neutral and professional tone.

These principles help improve consistency, reliability, and transparency in AI-generated responses.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Responsible AI is an ongoing process.

Future versions of ALTAIR may include:

* User feedback mechanisms
* Improved prompt evaluation
* More personalized roadmap generation
* Enhanced transparency around AI confidence and limitations
* Expanded accessibility and inclusivity features

---

# Our Responsibility

ALTAIR aims to demonstrate that AI can be integrated into educational products in a thoughtful, transparent, and human-centered way.

Success is not measured by replacing human expertise, but by helping learners make more informed decisions and confidently find their way forward.
