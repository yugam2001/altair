# User Personas

## Purpose

This document defines the primary users of **ALTAIR**. Understanding who the product is built for helps guide product decisions, user experience, AI prompt design, and feature prioritization.

Although ALTAIR can be used by anyone above the age of 15, the MVP focuses on learners who are making important education and career decisions.

---

# Primary Persona 1: High School Student

### Profile

* **Age:** 15–18
* **Education Level:** Class 10–12
* **Experience:** Limited career knowledge
* **Primary Goal:** Understand the pathway to a chosen career.

### Scenario

A Class 12 student wants to become a Software Engineer but is unsure about the next steps after school.

Questions they might have include:

* Which entrance exams should I prepare for?
* What are my higher education options?
* Which colleges should I consider?
* What should I focus on during college?
* Which specialization aligns with my career goal?

### Pain Points

* Information is scattered across multiple websites and videos.
* Difficulty identifying the correct sequence of steps.
* Overwhelmed by conflicting advice.
* Unsure where to begin.

### How ALTAIR Helps

ALTAIR generates a personalized roadmap that provides a structured overview of the student's journey based on their current education level, country or region, and career goal.

---

# Primary Persona 2: University Student

### Profile

* **Age:** 18–24
* **Education Level:** Undergraduate or postgraduate student
* **Experience:** Has chosen a field of study but wants career direction.
* **Primary Goal:** Prepare for internships, placements, or higher education.

### Scenario

A second-year Computer Science student wants to become an AI Engineer and wants to know which skills, projects, and learning priorities will best prepare them.

### Pain Points

* Too many online courses with no clear direction.
* Uncertainty about what employers value.
* Difficulty prioritizing learning.
* Lack of a structured learning plan.

### How ALTAIR Helps

ALTAIR creates a stage-aware roadmap that focuses on the student's current academic position and recommends practical next steps toward their career goal.

---

# Secondary Persona: Career Switcher

### Profile

* **Age:** 22+
* **Education Level:** Graduate or working professional
* **Experience:** Wants to transition into a different career.
* **Primary Goal:** Understand the most efficient pathway into a new profession.

### Scenario

A marketing professional wants to transition into Data Analytics but is unsure which skills to learn first or how to build a portfolio.

### Pain Points

* Unclear learning sequence.
* Information overload.
* Fear of wasting time on unnecessary resources.
* Balancing learning with work.

### How ALTAIR Helps

ALTAIR generates a personalized roadmap based on the learner's background, available study time, and career objective.

---

# Common User Goals

Across all personas, users want to:

* Understand the path toward a career goal.
* Know what to learn next.
* Reduce confusion caused by scattered information.
* Save time researching multiple sources.
* Build confidence through a structured plan.

---

# User Needs

The MVP should help users answer questions such as:

* Where do I start?
* What should I do next?
* Which milestones should I focus on?
* What skills are most important?
* What common mistakes should I avoid?

---

# Design Principles Derived from These Personas

To best serve these users, ALTAIR should:

* Keep the experience simple and beginner-friendly.
* Ask only the information necessary to generate a personalized roadmap.
* Adapt recommendations based on the user's current stage rather than assuming a one-size-fits-all journey.
* Present roadmaps in a structured and easy-to-follow format.
* Clearly communicate that AI-generated recommendations should be verified using official and trusted sources where applicable.

---

# Out of Scope for the MVP

The MVP intentionally does not aim to:

* Replace academic or career counselors.
* Provide official admission or legal guidance.
* Guarantee career outcomes.
* Recommend a single "best" college, university, or institution.
* Replace official information published by educational institutions.

Instead, ALTAIR serves as an intelligent guide that helps learners understand their options and confidently take the next step in their educational or professional journey.
